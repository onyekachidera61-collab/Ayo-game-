How to Play Ayo Olopon

  1. 1
    The Board: 12 pits (6 per player), each starting with 4 seeds. You control the 6 pits on your side.
  2. 2
    Your Turn: Pick any pit on your side with seeds. Pick them up and sow them anti-clockwise, one seed per pit.
  3. 3
    Capture: If your last seed lands on the opponent's side and that pit now has 2 or 3 seeds, you capture them! Continue capturing consecutive pits with 2 or 3 seeds.
  4. 4
    Win: Capture 25 or more seeds (out of 48 total). Most seeds when the game ends wins!
